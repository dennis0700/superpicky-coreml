Changes made by the superpicky-coreml project (indicated as required by CC BY-NC 4.0
section 3(a)(1)(B); conversion date, source version and upstream weights in SOURCE.txt):
- Loaded the released model.safetensors strictly and reparameterized the FastViT blocks.
- Converted to a Core ML ML Program (fp16) with coremltools 9.0; the ImageNet
  mean/std normalization is baked into the graph.
- No retraining or fine-tuning.

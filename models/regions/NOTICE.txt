Regional species lists (regions.json)

What it is: for each country and subnational region, the species-database ids (the
same numbering as birdtrace_class_map.json and species_names.json) of the birds on
its eBird checklist, so an app can restrict species identification to the region
where a photo was taken.

Source:    eBird, Cornell Lab of Ornithology (https://ebird.org), via SuperPicky
           (https://github.com/jamesphotography/SuperPicky): birdid/data/ebird_regions.db
           at commit 75a9321b1771553592bf13a4a3d930264e8cd0a3 (eBird lists fetched
           2026-09-17, eBird taxonomy 2025.0).

Changes made by the superpicky-coreml project (indicated as required by CC BY-NC-SA 4.0;
conversion date, source version and upstream weights in SOURCE.txt):
- The conversion started from cfanet_iaa_ava_res50_fp16.pth, the fp16 copy of the
  upstream weights distributed with SuperPicky
  (https://huggingface.co/jamesphotography/SuperPicky-models), not from the
  upstream fp32 file.
- Converted to a Core ML ML Program (fp16) with coremltools 9.0; the ImageNet
  mean/std normalization is baked into the graph.
- The bicubic resize of the positional embedding (not supported by coremltools 9.0)
  is replaced by a precomputed constant. The converted model therefore only
  accepts a fixed 384x384 input.
- No retraining or fine-tuning.
